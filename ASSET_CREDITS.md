# Card assets

The 52 face sprites are crops from `Poker cards 1.3/minicards.png` in the user-provided `Poker cards 1.3.zip` (41,603 bytes). The archive contains three PNG files and no author, license, or instructions document. No third-party authorship or license is asserted here. The original archive remains unchanged.

`back.png` is an original procedural pattern. Missing face/back assets are replaced at runtime by the procedural pixel-card renderer in `solitaire_screen.lua`.

PokePet resolves Pokemon art from the running game's existing sprite providers and enabled mods. No Pokemon sprite files are redistributed in its game assets. The PokePet documentation screenshots show Pikachu from 1025Dex's G9 Gen3 sprite option, imported from the user-provided `g9-battle-sprites-gen3-1.5.4.zip`; that pack's attribution and license remain with 1025Dex. Pokemon characters belong to their respective owners.
